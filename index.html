Recipes for Odin

A long long time ago on a far distant realm a weathered hand rolled a recipe scroll and placed it into a bottle. Casting it to the ocean it made a 10 year voyage before reaching the continent of Asgard. The bottle remained washed ashore for another millenia when Bestla happened to stumble upon it while collecting shells.

These became Odins favorite dishes.

  • Lamb Shanks to Die For
  • Tangy Chicken Stir Fry
  • Salmon with Thunder
  • An Omelette with a difference
